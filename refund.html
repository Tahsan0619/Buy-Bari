Refund & Return Policy

Not happy with your order? We'll make it right with a simple 7-day return process.

    Last updated: October 12, 2026

    At Buybari we want you to love what you buy. If something isn't right, this policy explains when you can return a product and how refunds work.

    Quick tip: Always check your parcel in front of the delivery person. If it's damaged, wrong or missing items, you can refuse it on the spot and call us. It's the fastest way to get it resolved.

    1. Return window

    You can request a return within 7 days of receiving your order.

    2. When you can return a product

    • The product is damaged, broken or defective on arrival;
    • You received the wrong item, size or colour;
    • Items are missing from your order;
    • The product is significantly different from its description on our website.

    3. Conditions for a return

    • The item must be unused, unwashed and in the same condition you received it;
    • Original packaging, tags, accessories, manuals and free gifts must be included;
    • Please share an unboxing video or clear photos of the issue. This helps us process your request quickly.

    4. Items that cannot be returned

    • Food, groceries, honey, dry fruits and other perishable items, unless damaged or expired on arrival;
    • Personal care and hygiene products, innerwear and hijabs once opened;
    • Products damaged through misuse, accidents or improper installation after delivery;
    • Items bought on clearance or marked "non-returnable" on the product page;
    • Change-of-mind returns for products that have been used.

    5. How to return a product

    1. Contact us within 7 days: call +880 1777-012681, email contactus@buybari.com or message our Facebook page with your order number.
    2. Share the details: tell us what went wrong and send photos or an unboxing video of the product.
    3. Get approval: our team will review your request, usually within 24-48 hours, and confirm the next steps.
    4. Send the product back: pack the item securely with all accessories. We'll arrange a pickup or tell you how to send it through a courier.
    5. Receive your replacement or refund: once we receive and inspect the item, we'll ship a replacement or process your refund.

    6. Refunds

    • Approved refunds are processed within 5-7 working days after we receive the returned product.
    • Cash on delivery orders are refunded through bKash, Nagad, Rocket or bank transfer, whichever you prefer.
    • Online payments are refunded to the original payment method where possible. Card refunds may take longer to appear depending on your bank.
    • If the return is due to our mistake (damaged, defective or wrong item), we cover the return delivery charge and refund the full amount, including delivery.
    • For other approved returns, the original delivery charge and return shipping cost may be deducted.

    7. Exchanges

    Want a different size or colour? Contact us within 7 days and we'll exchange it, subject to availability. If the new item has a different price, we'll collect or refund the difference.

    8. Order cancellations

    You can cancel an order free of charge before it is dispatched. If you have already paid online, we'll refund the full amount within 5-7 working days.

    9. Need help?

    Our support team is available Saturday to Thursday, 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Reach us at +880 1777-012681 or contactus@buybari.com, or visit our Contact page.
