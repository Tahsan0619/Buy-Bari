About Buybari

Your trusted online shop for everything your bari (home) needs, from Dhaka to every district of Bangladesh.

Our story

Bringing the market home, one parcel at a time

Buybari started with a simple idea: shopping for the home should be easy, honest and affordable. We noticed how much time families spent going from market to market for decor, kitchen tools, clothes and everyday essentials, often without knowing whether the quality or price was right.

So we built a single place where you can find carefully selected products, see clear prices, and order with just a phone call or a few taps. Every item is checked by our team before it leaves our Dhaka warehouse, and you can pay in cash only when the parcel reaches your door.

The name says it all. Buy + bari: buy for your home, with the comfort of home.
