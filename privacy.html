Privacy Policy

Your trust matters to us. Here is how we handle your personal information.

    Last updated: October 12, 2026

    This Privacy Policy explains what information Buybari collects when you visit our website or place an order, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website, you agree to the practices described here.

    1. Information we collect

    Information you give us:

    • Your name, phone number, email address (optional) and delivery address when you place an order;
    • Messages you send us through the contact form, phone, email or Facebook;
    • Details you provide when requesting a return or refund, such as photos of a product.

    Information collected automatically:

    • Basic device and browser information, pages visited and time spent, used to improve the website;
    • Your cart and wishlist, which are saved in your own browser's local storage so they are still there when you return.

    2. How we use your information

    • To process, confirm and deliver your orders;
    • To contact you about your order, delivery, returns or refunds;
    • To respond to your questions and provide customer support;
    • To prevent fraud and fake orders;
    • To improve our products, website and services;
    • To send you offers and updates, only if you have agreed to receive them. You can opt out at any time.

    3. Sharing your information

    We do not sell or rent your personal information. We only share what is necessary with:

    • Courier partners: your name, phone number and address, so they can deliver your parcel;
    • Payment providers: such as bKash, Nagad, Rocket or card processors, to complete your payment. We never see or store your full card details or PIN;
    • Authorities: when required by the laws of Bangladesh or to protect our rights and our customers' safety.

    4. Cookies & local storage

    Our website uses your browser's local storage to remember your cart and wishlist. We may also use cookies or similar tools to understand how visitors use the site. You can clear these at any time from your browser settings; doing so will empty your saved cart and wishlist.

    5. Data security

    We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

    6. How long we keep your data

    We keep order information for as long as needed to provide our services, handle returns and warranty claims, and meet legal and accounting requirements. After that, it is deleted or anonymised.

    7. Your rights

    You may ask us to:

    • access the personal information we hold about you;
    • correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
    • delete your information, where we are not required to keep it by law;
    • stop sending you promotional messages.

    To make a request, contact us using the details below.

    8. Children's privacy

    Our website is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us their information, please contact us and we will remove it.

    9. Third-party links

    Our website may link to other sites, such as our Facebook page or payment gateways. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites, and we encourage you to read their policies.

    10. Changes to this policy

    We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with the updated date shown at the top.
